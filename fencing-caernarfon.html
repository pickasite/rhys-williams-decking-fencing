Why our fencing in Caernarfon
is built to last.

When you are looking for professional **fencing in Caernarfon**, the most important thing to consider is how the boundary is actually constructed. Most "off-the-shelf" panels are held together by thin staples and a flimsy frame. In a coastal gale off the Menai Strait, those panels act like sails and snap at the post. Our **Fencing Caernarfon** service is different because we build "Closeboard" or "Feather-edge" fences board-by-board, directly on your site.

Rhys Williams provides the premier service for fencing for Caernarfon homeowners, utilizing on-site construction and UC4-rated timber. We don't use weak pre-made panels; we engineer every boundary in LL55 to be a single, unified structure that handles the high-velocity winds of the Gwynedd coast.

By building your new fence on-site, we create a boundary that is far more robust than anything you'll find in a shop. Each vertical board is nailed to heavy-duty horizontal rails, which are then secured into 100mm pressure-treated posts. This method of **fencing for Caernarfon** residents allows for much better wind distribution and prevents the "rattle and snap" common with cheap alternatives. Whether you are in Bontnewydd, Llanrug, or Waunfawr, we provide a boundary that actually stays where it's put.

Quality Timber for the Gwynedd Climate

The secret to long-lasting **fencing in Caernarfon** is the quality of the timber. We exclusively use UC4-grade pressure-treated wood. Unlike standard treatment, UC4 is specifically rated for permanent ground contact. In our wet climate, standard fence posts rot from the inside out; our builds utilize superior protection to ensure a 15-20 year lifespan.
